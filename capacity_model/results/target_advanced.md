# What it takes: 300 MNOK/yr retrofit on advanced (DP2) projects

Target: **30 retrofit DP2 projects per year in years 3–5** (= 300 MNOK). Today: 3 DP2 per year in total, ≈ 1.2 retrofit (retrofit share 40%, placeholder), from ≈ 3.3 opportunities.

Assumptions: DP2 = 3.5 × a simple project in own hours; traded sensors 75% of the price (180 MNOK/yr of sensor purchases at the target), 9–13 weeks lead time. With learning. DP2 market × M reached linearly by year 3; other volumes unchanged. 40 runs per point.
